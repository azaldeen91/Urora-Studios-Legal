NotiPocket Privacy Policy

Urora Studios · Effective date: September 15, 2026

NotiPocket is a notification history application designed to save notifications on the user's device so they can be reviewed later. Urora Studios is committed to privacy and to keeping notification content under the user's control.

Information NotiPocket processes

When the user explicitly grants Android Notification Access, NotiPocket may process notification information made available by Android, including the originating app, notification title or sender, notification text, and date/time. This access is required for the app's core notification-history functionality.

Local storage

Saved notification content is stored locally on the user's device for the app's notification-history features. NotiPocket does not operate a server for uploading or storing saved notification content.

Data sharing and sale

Urora Studios does not sell saved notification content and does not share saved notification content with advertisers or data brokers. When a user intentionally uses a share feature, the selected content is sent only through the app or service chosen by the user.

Permissions and device features

NotiPocket uses Android Notification Access only after the user enables it in system settings. Optional PIN and biometric features may be used to protect access to the app. Biometric authentication is handled through Android's system authentication mechanisms; NotiPocket does not receive or store fingerprint or biometric templates.

User controls and deletion

Users can manage saved notification history within the app, including deleting saved items or clearing saved history. Notification Access can be revoked at any time through Android system settings. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored application data according to Android's normal app-uninstallation behavior.

Children

NotiPocket is not specifically directed to children. The app's intended audience and Google Play declarations are maintained in accordance with applicable Google Play requirements.

Security

We use reasonable app-level safeguards and privacy-oriented design to protect locally stored information. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when NotiPocket's features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when appropriate.

Contact
