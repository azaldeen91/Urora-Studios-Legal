Chai Chai Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 · Android game version 0.2.4

English · العربية

Chai Chai is a cafe management game provided by Urora Studios. This policy explains how the current Android version handles information. Its package name is com.urorastudios.chaichai.

Information used on your device

The game processes your gameplay input and stores progress locally, including coins, purchases and upgrades, staff, tables, orders, player position, tasks, tutorial progress, and language, sound and vibration preferences. Customers, orders and money shown in the game are fictional gameplay records, not real customer or payment information.

The game does not require an account or sign-in. It does not access your contacts, messages, notifications from other apps, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Collection, transmission and sharing

The current game does not transmit your local save or gameplay activity to Urora Studios or an app backend. It has no internet permission, cloud-save service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated crash-reporting SDK. Urora Studios does not sell or share your local game save with advertisers or data brokers. No real-money purchases or advertisements are integrated in this version.

Permissions

The game uses Android's vibration permission for optional gameplay feedback. Vibration can be disabled in the game settings. This permission does not give access to personal information.

Local storage, security and deletion

Progress and recovery copies are stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app storage controls. Automatic Android app-data backup is disabled. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Local progress remains until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it. To delete all local saves, recovery copies and settings, open Android Settings → Apps → Chai Chai → Storage → Clear storage (the labels may vary by device). This permanently resets your progress. Uninstalling normally also removes the app's private data. Urora Studios has no server copy to restore or delete.

Platform services and this website

Google Play and Android may process installation and diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings. This is separate from an analytics or crash-upload service embedded by us. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting it is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. No tracking scripts or forms have been added to this page.

Contact and privacy requests

Contact Urora Studios at Zigzag3dprint@gmail.com for privacy questions. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send and use it to handle your request. Avoid sending passwords or unnecessary personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence; retention is limited to handling your request and any applicable legal obligations.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our data practices change, including if future versions introduce accounts, online services, advertising or analytics.
